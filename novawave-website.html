How It Works

From Sunlight to
Your Switchboard

01

Generate

Our solar farm captures Warri's abundant sunlight through 5,000+ Tier-1 panels during peak daytime hours, generating clean electricity for immediate use and battery charging.

02

Store

Excess solar energy is stored in our LiFePO4 battery bank — providing a 2-day initial buffer that expands to 5–7 days as the system scales. Continuous supply guaranteed, day and night.

03

Distribute

Clean energy flows through our medium-voltage distribution network to your home or business via a smart prepaid meter. Monitor usage, top up, and track savings — all from your phone.
